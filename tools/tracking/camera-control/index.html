Absolute pan & tilt

Logitech extension

Reading capability…

Only Move to target sends movement. Driver read-back is not proof of arrival, settling or millimetre precision. Recheck field references after any move.

Pan & tilt

Bounded jog pulses

Arrow keys move the selected camera; + / − zoom; Esc sends stop. Disabled while editing inputs. Shift gives a longer pulse. Very short pulses may not overcome motor startup. Stop during absolute travel has not been physically verified.

Changing pan, tilt, zoom or focus can invalidate tracking calibration. Recheck the reference markers afterward.

Exposure test shortcuts

Manual · nominal shutter time

These change exposure only. Add light or adjust gain separately; no automatic test preset is applied.

Research findings & capture limits

1080p30 is the verified capture path. No reproducible PTZ Pro 2-specific FPS unlock was found in SDK, firmware or open-source control research.

  • Smaller USB modes, including 160 × 120, still advertise at most 30 fps.
  • 2304 × 1296 / 1536 is advertised at 2 fps over raw USB, but Windows rejected capture. Extra optical detail is unverified.
  • The Logitech Software Reference Kit supports PTZ integration; no published FPS-unlock feature was found.
  • Absolute positioning reports a nominal 1° step on the inspected camera. Keep the camera fixed during tracking.
  • Native-mode, RightLight, crop and compression extensions are displayed read-only below. Unknown values are not safe tuning controls.
  • Short exposure, steady light, fixed focus and accurate timestamps remain the useful tracking improvements. 30 fps at 2 m/s gives 66.7 mm between samples, not 66.7 mm position error.

Sources: Logitech specifications · Absolute PTZ update · Software Reference Kit
